# Mission 05 — Ghosts in the Logs

## Briefing
Nightjar’s archive contains references to Xid and ECC concepts, but your laptop may never generate them—and that is a good thing.

## Objective
Learn how GPU hardware/driver fault evidence differs from ordinary workload failure.

## Method
Use official/reference or clearly synthetic log excerpts for Xid/ECC cases. Build a small casebook: what the signal means at a high level, what surrounding evidence you would collect, what not to conclude from the code alone, and when hardware escalation is appropriate.

## Evidence
- Xid/ECC triage table
- example investigation flow
- clear label separating simulated evidence from your real system

## Victory condition
You can discuss serious GPU fault telemetry without pretending you induced hardware faults in a home lab.
