# Final — Nightjar Autopsy

## Briefing
A fresh Nightjar run fails overnight. You are given the stored telemetry, logs, workload output, and no witness testimony.

## Challenge format
Have another person or tool select 3 safe conditions from your fault deck and apply them during a run without revealing the sequence. One condition may be a telemetry failure rather than a workload failure.

## Required report
1. Executive incident summary.
2. Timeline of events.
3. Evidence for each hypothesis considered.
4. Root cause vs contributing factors.
5. What monitoring detected, missed, or falsely suggested.
6. Corrective action.
7. Prevention/alerting improvements.

## Victory condition
Your report lets a reader reconstruct the incident without being present and makes clear which statements are facts, inferences, or unknowns.

Blackbox-GPU is complete when telemetry stops being a dashboard and becomes evidence.
